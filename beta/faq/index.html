FAQ

Tester questions

About the beta itself. For how Spends works day to day, see the product help and FAQ.

Is my data safe?

Your spends, alerts and settings are stored encrypted on your phone, with cloud backup off. There is no bank login. You can turn on App lock in Settings, and Settings → Delete all data erases everything. One caveat: beta builds are debug builds, so keep USB debugging off when you aren't using it.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. SMS, notifications and transactions never leave your phone. The diagnostic log leaves only when you tap Share diagnostic log and send it yourself, and it holds no amounts, merchants or message text. See exactly what it contains.

Can I use both builds?

Not side by side: they are the same app, so only one can be installed at a time. You can move from the easy build to the full build by installing over it (with adb, or a link if your phone allows it) and your data stays.

Will my data carry over to the Play version?

We can't promise that yet. Android keeps data only when the new app is signed with the same key, and the Play version may be signed differently. If Android won't install over your beta, you'd have to uninstall first, which deletes the data. Export a copy first in Settings → Export transactions (CSV or JSON). There is no import yet.

How do I leave the beta?

Uninstall Spends (press and hold the icon → App info → Uninstall). That removes all its data. If you joined Play testing, you can also leave from the Play testing link. Let us know, so we stop sending you builds.

Why does Play Protect warn me?

Play Protect warns about any app installed from outside the Play Store. The easy build asks for no SMS or notification access, so it installs after the warning. The full build does ask for them, and in India Play Protect blocks such apps outright when they come from a link. That is why the full build goes through Play testing.

Which banks work?

Spends recognises SMS from about 20 banks by sender, including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union Bank, IndusInd, IDFC First and Federal. The full list is in Known issues. If yours isn't there, tell us.

It missed a transaction.

In the easy build, Spends only knows what you share, so share that alert or tap + in Activity. In the full build, open the app (it checks new bank SMS when it opens), then try Settings → Data sources → Sync financial SMS now. If it still isn't there, send the diagnostic log and tell us the bank and how you paid.

It got the amount wrong.

In Activity, tap the spend to delete it, and add the right one with +. Then tell us: the bank, what it showed and what it should have been. The alert text helps most if you're comfortable sharing it with account digits removed. How to report.

Will it drain my battery?

It shouldn't, and we'd like to know if it does. The opposite problem is more common: battery savers on some phones stop the full build from catching notifications. Battery settings for Samsung and Xiaomi.
