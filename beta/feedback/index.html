Feedback

Tell us what it got wrong

Wrong amounts, wrong merchants and missed payments are the most useful things you can report. Small annoyances count too.

Send feedback from the app

  1. Open Settings

    Tap the gear icon at the top right of any tab.

  2. Find Diagnostics

    Scroll down to Diagnostics. It shows your tester ID (like t-3f9a2c) and how many events are recorded.

  3. Tap Share diagnostic log

    Android's share sheet opens with a file named spends-diagnostics-<tester ID>-<date>.json. Pick WhatsApp, Gmail or whichever app you use to reach us.

  4. Add a few lines and send

    Send it to the person who invited you to the beta. Say what happened in your own words. A screenshot helps.

What a good report has

  • What you expected, and what happened instead. “The Swiggy order showed as ₹45, it was ₹450” beats “amounts are wrong”.
  • Your bank, and whether the spend came from an SMS, a payment notification, a shared alert or something you typed.
  • Which build: the beta number and easy or full, from the file you installed.
  • A screenshot. Blur or crop amounts and names if you like; we mostly need to see the screen.
  • If an alert was misread, the alert text helps most. Send it only if you are comfortable, with account digits removed. The diagnostic log never includes it.

What the diagnostic log contains

The log is always on and stays on your phone, stored encrypted. It holds the most recent 2,000 events. It leaves your phone only when you tap Share diagnostic log. Clear log, on the same screen, empties it.

It contains

  • Event codes with their times, like a sync finishing or a screen opening.
  • Counts, durations and error codes. Error messages are scrubbed first.
  • App version, beta number, commit and which build (easy or full).
  • Android and WebView versions, screen size, text size, light or dark mode and language. No phone model or device ID.
  • How many spends you have and where they came from (SMS, notifications, typed, example), how many messages couldn't be read and how old your newest and oldest data is.
  • Which bank's alerts failed to read, and why, as a code and a count.
  • Whether pay is set up, your payday date, how many goals you have, whether app lock is on.
  • Your tester ID: a random code made on your phone, not your name.

It never contains

  • Amounts, balances or your salary
  • Merchant or payee names
  • SMS or notification text
  • Account or card numbers
  • UPI IDs, phone numbers or email addresses
  • Your name or contacts

This is enforced in the app when each event is written, not left to each screen: anything that looks like money or a name is blanked.

What to test each week

  • Open Activity and compare a few spends with your bank app: amount, merchant and date.
  • Look for anything missing: a UPI payment, a card spend, a refund or your salary.
  • Look for anything doubled: the same payment showing twice.
  • Read Pulse. Does Spend health match how the month actually feels?
  • Try the one action Pulse suggests, or tell us why you wouldn't.
  • Around payday: check that the new cycle starts on the right day.
  • Share the diagnostic log once, with a line on how the week went.
