Install in four steps

  1. Download the app on your Android phone

    Tap the button above. Your browser may say the file can be harmful; that's its standard warning for apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.

  2. Open it and allow the install

    Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app, then tap Install.

  3. If Play Protect asks to scan the app, let it

    This build doesn't read your SMS or notifications, so Play Protect lets it install.

  4. Open Spends and set up your month

    Tell it your salary and payday, or explore with example data first.

Adding your spends

This test build can't read your messages by itself, so you send it bank alerts. It takes a few seconds each:

  1. Open your Messages app and find a bank or UPI alert (a debit or credit message).

  2. Press and hold the message, then tap Share (in some apps it's under ⋮).

  3. Pick Spends. It reads the amount, merchant and date. Tap Add spend.

You can also copy an alert and paste it in Activity → SMS, or add a spend by hand with +.

Why not read SMS automatically?

Android blocks apps that read SMS or notifications when they're installed from a link instead of the Play Store (“App blocked to protect your device”). Spends will read bank SMS automatically once it's on the Play Store. Until then, sharing alerts gives it the same information, and nothing leaves your phone.

If the person who invited you asked you to try the SMS-reading build, they'll send you a Play Store testing link for it.

Your data

  • The alerts you share are read and stored on your phone only. No bank login, no upload.
  • Spends can't see your messages, contacts or notifications. It only gets what you share.
  • The app keeps a diagnostic log of app events (screens, syncs, errors, counts) on your phone. It never contains amounts, merchants, messages, names or account numbers, and it leaves your phone only if you tap Share diagnostic log.
  • Settings → Delete all data removes everything.

Found a problem?

Open Settings → Diagnostics → Share diagnostic log and send the file to the person who invited you, with a line on what you were doing and what you expected. Screenshots help too.

This is a test build. Numbers may be wrong in places, screens will change, and you may need to reinstall for the next version. Please don't rely on it for money decisions yet.
