How to installAll releases

Two builds, one app

Every release has two APKs. They are the same app. The difference is how your spends get in.

Start here

Easy build

Installs from a link. Play Protect lets it through because it asks for no SMS or notification access.

  • You share bank alerts into it: in Messages, press and hold an alert, tap Share, pick Spends.
  • Or paste an alert, or add a spend by hand with +.
  • Spends knows only what you send it, so Pulse is as complete as what you share.
Ask us for it

Full build

Reads bank SMS and payment notifications by itself, on your phone. This is how Spends is meant to work.

  • Play Protect blocks it when installed from a link, with no way past the warning.
  • So it comes through Google Play internal testing, or adb for technical testers.
  • On first open it can scan the bank SMS already on your phone to fill in your recent spends.

Your privacy during the beta

  • Your spends, alerts and settings stay on your phone, stored encrypted. Nothing is sent to us.
  • The app keeps a diagnostic log of app events (screens, syncs, counts, errors). It never contains amounts, merchants, message text, names or account numbers.
  • That log leaves your phone only when you tap Share diagnostic log and choose where to send it.
  • Settings → Delete all data erases everything on the phone.
