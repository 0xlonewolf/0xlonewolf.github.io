Install

Get Spends on your phone

You need an Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer. Open this page on that phone. Start with the easy build: it installs from a link in a few minutes.

The easy build

  1. Download the latest easy build

    Tap the button below. Your browser may say the file can be harmful. That is its standard warning for apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.

    Easy build · beta 27 · 6.3 MB

  2. Allow installs from your browser

    Open the downloaded file from the notification, or from Files → Downloads. Android will ask you to allow Install unknown apps for your browser (or Files app). Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, and tap Install.

    You can turn that permission off again once Spends is installed.

  3. Let Play Protect check it

    Play Protect may ask to scan the app, or say it doesn't recognise it. Let it scan. If it shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. The exact words differ from phone to phone.

    The easy build asks for no SMS or notification access, so Play Protect lets it install. If you see “App blocked to protect your device” with no way past it, you have downloaded the full build by mistake.

  4. Open Spends and choose how it sees your spends

    The first screen asks how Spends should get your spends. In the easy build you can pick:

    • Share bank alerts to Spends, then Set up my month (your pay and payday). This is the one to choose.
    • Add spends myself, if you would rather type them in.
    • Explore with example data, to look around first. You can switch to your own data in one tap.

    The full build shows “Read bank SMS on this phone” at the top instead, as in the picture.

  5. Send it your first bank alert

    In your Messages app, press and hold a bank or UPI alert, tap Share (in some apps it is under the three-dot menu), and pick Spends. Check the amount, merchant and date, then tap Add spend.

    You can also copy an alert and paste it: in Activity, tap SMS. Or tap + to add a spend by hand.

The full build

The full build reads bank SMS and payment notifications by itself. In India, Play Protect blocks apps that ask for that access when they are installed from a link, and there is no override. So the full build comes through Google Play instead.

Through Play internal testing (most people)

Message us the Gmail address you use on your phone. We'll add you as a tester and send you the Play link. You then install Spends from the Play Store like any other app.

On first open, choose Read bank SMS on this phone and tap Scan my bank SMS. Spends explains what it reads before Android asks for permission. To catch UPI payments as they happen, open Settings → Data sources and tap Turn on notification access.

The Play build may be signed differently from the link builds. If Play says it can't install over the easy build, you will need to uninstall the easy build first, which deletes its data. Export it first in Settings → Export transactions if you want a copy.

With adb (technical testers)

If you have a computer with Android platform tools and USB debugging on, ask the person who invited you for the full build APK (it isn't published on this site) and run:

adb install -r -g spends-beta-N-full-xxxxxxx.apk

-r installs over an existing copy and keeps its data. -g grants the runtime permissions it asks for. Notification access is a separate switch: turn it on in Spends under Settings → Data sources.

The link builds share one app ID and signing key, so the full build installs over the easy build and keeps your data.

Updating to a new build

Download the new APK from Release notes and install it over the old one. Don't uninstall first. Beta builds are signed with the same key, so Android treats the new one as an update and keeps your data.

If Android says “App not installed” or that the package conflicts, your old copy was signed differently (this can happen with the earliest builds). Uninstall it, then install the new one. Uninstalling deletes the data on the phone.

If you use the Play testing build, updates come through the Play Store.

Uninstalling

Press and hold the Spends icon, tap App info, then Uninstall. Everything Spends stored is removed with it; cloud backup is off, so nothing comes back on reinstall. To wipe the data but keep the app, use Settings → Delete all data.

Stop your phone from killing capture

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. That matters for the full build, which listens for payment notifications. If spends only show up after you open the app, change these settings. Menu names vary by phone and Android version.

Samsung

  • Settings → Apps → Spends → Battery: choose Unrestricted.
  • Settings → Battery → Background usage limits: make sure Spends is not under Sleeping or Deep sleeping apps.

Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco

  • Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Spends: turn on Autostart.
  • In the same screen, Battery saver: choose No restrictions.
  • Optionally, in the recent apps view, press and hold Spends and lock it.

The easy build only gets the alerts you share, so these settings matter much less there. More on what else can go wrong in Known issues.
