Known issues

What doesn't work yet

The honest list. If you hit one of these, you don't need to report it, but your details still help.

  • The easy build can't read SMS by itself

    Android blocks link-installed apps that read SMS or notifications, so the easy build only knows the alerts you share or type in. Pulse is only as complete as what you send it.

    What to do: Share alerts from Messages (press and hold → Share → Spends), or ask us for the full build through Play internal testing.

  • Some phones stop background capture

    Samsung, Xiaomi and other phones can stop apps in the background to save battery. The full build then misses payment notifications until you open it. We haven't tested this on real phones yet.

    What to do: Set Spends's battery use to unrestricted and allow autostart. Steps are in Install. Opening the app also checks for new bank SMS.

  • About 20 banks are recognised

    Spends recognises bank SMS by sender from HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union Bank, IndusInd, IDFC First, Federal, RBL, AU, Bandhan, Bank of India, Indian Bank, Central Bank and IDBI. Alerts from other banks and wallets may be read less reliably or not at all.

    What to do: Add a missed spend with +, and tell us your bank so we can add it.

  • Payday cash forecast needs a balance and a safety floor

    The forecast on Pulse shows “Not enough data” until you have entered a bank balance in the last 3 days and set a safety floor. That's on purpose. Its thresholds are still an experiment.

    What to do: Tap Add balance on Pulse, set a safety floor in Settings → Payday forecast, and tell us if the forecast looks wrong.

  • Device testing is still partial

    Every screen and journey passes our automated tests, but the real-phone checks aren't finished. Fingerprint lock, the home-screen widget, real UPI notifications, Share from real Messages apps, battery limits and TalkBack still need checking on real phones.

    What to do: If any of these misbehave on your phone, you're finding exactly what we need. Send the diagnostic log.

  • Bank alert formats change

    Banks change their SMS wording. A new layout can be skipped or misread (a wrong merchant or a wrong date) until we add it.

    What to do: In the full build, after updating, run Settings → Data sources → Scan full SMS archive to re-read older messages. Report misreads on Feedback.

  • Test builds are debuggable

    The beta APKs are debug builds. Someone with your unlocked phone, USB debugging and a computer could read the app's data.

    What to do: Keep USB debugging off when you're not using it, and turn on App lock in Settings.

  • Looks and dark mode are a work in progress

    We're still polishing screens, especially in dark mode and with large system text.

    What to do: Tell us the two or three screens you like least, with a screenshot.
