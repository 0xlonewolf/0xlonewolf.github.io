Delete your Spends account

Spends works without an account. If you signed in with Google, here’s how to remove it.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Spends → Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account is removed from our server straight away.
  • To erase everything on your phone as well (transactions, settings), use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.

Without the app

  • A contact email for deletion requests will be listed here before Spends is released on Google Play. Until then, use the in-app option.

What is deleted

  • Your Google account ID and email, your free-period and purchase-check records, your plan status and purchase links: everything our server holds about you. We keep no copies.
  • Your spending data was never on our server; it lives only on your phone.

What isn't

  • A Google Play subscription keeps running until you cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions. Google keeps its own purchase records under its policies.
  • If you joined the Pro waitlist, ask us to remove that email too.
