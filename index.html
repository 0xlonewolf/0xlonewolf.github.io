Your money stays on your phone.

Spends works out everything on the phone itself. There is no bank login, and your transactions, messages and balances are never sent to us or anyone else.

What it reads

  • Bank and UPI SMS on this phone
  • Payment notifications, if you allow them
  • Alerts you share into Spends, and spends you add

What it never reads

  • OTPs and personal chats
  • Your contacts
  • Your bank login or UPI PIN

What Spends refuses to do

  • Hold your data hostage: export or delete it any time
  • Sell loans, credit or your data
  • Paywall the warning that you might run short

Questions, answered plainly.

All questions →

Does Spends upload my SMS or transactions?

No. Messages are read and worked out on your phone. Spends has no bank login and never sends your transactions, messages or balances to a server.

Why does it need SMS permission?

Your bank already texts you every debit and credit. Reading those alerts is how Spends sees your spending without a bank login. You can say no and add spends yourself, or share alerts into the app one by one.

Which banks work?

About 20 Indian banks today, including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis and Kotak, plus UPI apps and wallets. More are being added.

Is it really free?

Yes. Automatic capture, Spend health, the cycle rail and the run-short warning are free forever. Pro, ₹199 a month, adds forecasts and coaching, and is a waitlist for now.

Is Spends on iPhone?

No. iPhone doesn’t let apps read SMS, so Spends is Android only.
