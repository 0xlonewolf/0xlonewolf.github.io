Privacy Policy

Region: India (Play Store) · Last updated July 2026 · Spends is a local-first financial decision-support tool for Android.

Data we process

  • ·Bank & UPI notifications (primary, optional): when you enable the notification listener, the app reads alerts from approved financial apps. Only a short snippet (max 80 characters) and a content hash are stored.
  • ·Financial SMS (secondary, optional): parsed on-device when you grant SMS permission after in-app disclosure.
  • ·SMS history import (optional, one-time): last 90 days only, on-device.
  • ·Manual data: income baseline, goals, transactions you enter, and intervention feedback.
  • ·Analytics: local event counts only — never uploaded.
  • ·Diagnostic log: the app records app events on your device (screens opened, syncs, errors, counts, device and OS version). It never contains amounts, merchants, message text, names or account numbers, and it leaves your device only when you tap “Share diagnostic log” and choose where to send it.

Marketing & Pro waitlist

  • ·If you join the Pro waitlist (website or in-app), we collect only the email you submit.
  • ·Email is sent over HTTPS to Formspree so we can notify founding members when Pro opens.
  • ·Waitlist email is not stored in the app’s on-device financial database.
  • ·We never send financial transactions, SMS, or notification content with the waitlist form.

Third-party processors

  • ·Formspree (formspree.io) — processes Pro waitlist email submissions only.
  • ·No third-party analytics, advertising, or financial-data processors in the default build.

Data we do not collect

  • ·We do not upload SMS or transaction data to Spends servers.
  • ·We do not sell or share financial data with advertisers.
  • ·We do not read OTP or non-financial personal messages.
  • ·We do not upload or parse bank account statements (PDF/CSV).

Storage & retention

  • ·On Android, financial data is stored in encrypted on-device storage (AndroidX EncryptedSharedPreferences). Cloud backup is disabled. Data persists until you use Settings → Delete all data or uninstall.
  • ·Waitlist emails are retained until Pro launches (plus a reasonable outreach period). Request deletion via Contact.

Your rights

  • ·Access — view financial data in Transactions and Settings.
  • ·Correction — edit categories and merchants inline.
  • ·Deletion — Settings → Delete all data (irreversible). For waitlist email deletion, contact us.

Contact

  • ·Privacy questions and waitlist deletion requests: open an issue in the Finance repository or contact the maintainer listed in Play Console / 0xlonewolf.github.io.

We will update this policy before Play Store releases that change data practices. Spends is not directed at children under 18.
