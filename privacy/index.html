Privacy Policy

Region: India · Last updated October 2026 · Spends is a local-first spending-awareness app for Android.

Your spending data stays on your phone

  • ·Bank & UPI notifications (optional): when you allow notification access, Spends reads alerts from approved bank and payment apps. It stores only a short snippet (max 80 characters), a content hash and the parsed amount, merchant and date.
  • ·Financial SMS (optional): after an in-app explanation and your permission, Spends reads bank and payment SMS on your phone, including your past messages if you choose to import them. OTPs and personal messages are skipped.
  • ·What you enter: your payday and usual spending, goals, transactions, and which cards belong to which account.
  • ·All of this is stored encrypted on your phone (Android Keystore-backed storage, cloud backup off). It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • ·Usage counts and the diagnostic log are kept on your phone too. The diagnostic log contains no amounts, merchants, message text, names or account numbers, and leaves your phone only if you tap “Share diagnostic log”.

Optional account (Continue with Google)

  • ·You can use Spends without an account. If you sign in with Google, we store: your Google account ID, your email address, when your free period started, how many free purchase checks you've used, your plan status from Google Play, and when you signed up.
  • ·Why: so your free period and plan carry over if you reinstall or change phones, and so we can confirm your subscription with Google Play.
  • ·Where: our server on Google Cloud in Mumbai, India (region asia-south1).
  • ·How long: until you delete your account. Deleting removes it from our database straight away.
  • ·We never store your transactions, SMS, notifications or anything you track in Spends on the server.

Payments

  • ·Subscriptions are bought and billed through Google Play, which handles your UPI, card or other payment details under Google's own privacy policy. We never see them.
  • ·From Google Play we receive only your subscription status, the product and an anonymous purchase reference.
  • ·Cancel anytime in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Pro waitlist

  • ·If you join the waitlist (website or app), we store only the email you enter, on our server in Mumbai, India, to tell you when Pro opens.
  • ·Ask us to remove it at any time (see Contact).

Reminders

  • ·Weekly-check, free-period and trial reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data is sent to us.

What we don't do

  • ·We don't upload your SMS, notifications or transactions.
  • ·We don't sell or share personal data, and we use no advertising or third-party analytics.
  • ·We don't read OTPs or personal messages.

Your rights (India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act)

  • ·Access and correction: see and edit everything in the app. For your account details, contact us.
  • ·Erasure: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account from our server; Settings → Delete all data erases everything on your phone. You can also request account deletion on the web: /delete-account.
  • ·Withdraw consent: sign out and delete your account, or switch off SMS and notification access in Android settings at any time.
  • ·Grievances: contact us (below). We'll respond promptly and within the time the law requires.

Contact and grievance officer

  • ·A contact email will be listed here before Spends is released on Google Play.

We will update this policy before Play Store releases that change data practices. Spends is not directed at children under 18.
