Help and FAQ

Answers, in plain words.

Installing, banks, permissions, fixing numbers, the forecast, and your data.

Getting set up

How do I install Spends?

Spends is in beta on Android. Join the beta and follow the steps on the install page. Then see Getting started for setup, step by step. Spends is Android only, because iPhone doesn't let apps read SMS.

Which banks are supported?

About 20 Indian banks are recognised by their SMS sender: HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara, Union Bank, IndusInd, IDFC FIRST, Federal, RBL, AU, Bandhan, Bank of India, Indian Bank, Central Bank and IDBI. UPI apps and wallets are read too.

Alerts from other banks are read with general patterns. Amounts usually come through, but the merchant name may be missing. More banks are coming.

Why does Spends need SMS permission?

Every Indian bank already sends an SMS for every transaction. Reading those on your phone is how Spends sees your spending without a bank login or a statement upload. It reads only bank and UPI alerts, and skips OTPs, personal chats and contacts.

You can say no. Spends still works if you add spends by hand or share alerts into it.

Does Spends upload anything?

Not your spending. Your transactions, SMS and notifications stay on your phone, encrypted. The only things that can reach our server are an optional account (your Google ID, email and plan) and the Pro waitlist email, if you join it. See Security.

Fixing your numbers

An amount or merchant is wrong. How do I correct it?

Open Activity, tap the spend, and change the merchant or category, or mark it as not counted. Spends keeps your correction and uses it from then on. If an amount is wrong, tell us through beta feedback so the bank's format can be fixed.

Some transactions are missing.
  • New SMS are read when you open Spends. Open the app and give it a moment.
  • Check that SMS permission is still on in Android settings.
  • Some banks don't send an SMS for every payment. Turn on notification access so Spends also reads your bank and UPI app alerts.
  • Cash, and anything with no alert, needs adding by hand: tap + in Activity.
  • If your bank isn't in the list above, its alerts may not be read well yet. Please tell us.
I see the same payment twice.

Spends merges a bank SMS and a UPI notification for the same payment. If one slips through, tap it in Activity and mark it as not counted. If Spends flags a “possible duplicate charge” (same amount, same merchant, within 15 minutes), check your bank: it may really have charged you twice.

How are refunds handled?

A refund is linked to its purchase if it arrives within 30 days. A partial refund lowers that spend; a full refund removes it from your totals. A refund with no matching purchase never lowers your spend.

I have several accounts and cards.

Spends reads them all. In Wallet, drop each card on the account that pays it, so its spending rolls into that account. Moving money between your own accounts, and paying your card bill, isn't counted as spending.

Payday and the forecast

My payday isn't on the 1st, or my income varies.

Set any day from 1 to 31 as your payday in Settings → Pay cycle. Your cycle runs from that day to the day before the next payday. On Pro, the cycle also follows the day your salary actually lands, when it arrives up to 5 days early or late.

Spends is built around a regular salary today. If your income varies (freelance, gig, business), set your usual monthly amount and a typical day; proper support for irregular income is planned.

The forecast says “Not enough data”.

It refuses to guess. The card says what it needs. Usually one of these:

  • a safety floor (the least you want in the bank): tap Set safety floor;
  • a balance from the last 72 hours: from a recent bank alert, or tap Add balance;
  • a sync in the last 24 hours: open Spends;
  • at least a week of spending this cycle.

Your data

How do I export my data?

Settings → Export CSV or Export JSON. Free, always.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data erases everything on the phone. If you made an account, Settings → Account → Delete account removes it from our server, or use the web form. Uninstalling also removes the data on the phone.

I'm switching phones.

Today your data does not move by itself: Android cloud backup is off for Spends, on purpose. On the new phone, install Spends and let it scan your bank SMS again (if your messages moved across), and export from the old phone to keep a copy. Encrypted backups to your own Google Drive folder or a file, which only you can restore, are coming.

Phone settings

Spends misses alerts on my Xiaomi, Samsung or other phone.

Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery, which can stop notification access from working. Names vary by phone and Android version, but look for:

  • Battery: set Spends to “No restrictions” or “Unrestricted”.
  • On Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO: allow Autostart for Spends.
  • On Samsung: make sure Spends isn't in “Sleeping apps”.
  • Check that notification access is still on for Spends.

SMS are read whenever you open Spends, so opening the app catches up on anything missed.
