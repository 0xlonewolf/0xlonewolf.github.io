Spend health and the cycle rail

Pulse opens with one number: your Spend health, out of 100. It looks at your pace, the risk of running short, categories drifting above usual, and how much goes on small spends. The app shows what pulled the score down, so you can see why it isn't higher.

Below it is the cycle rail: one cell per day, from payday to the next payday. Green is spent at your usual pace. Marigold is spent ahead of pace. Pale cells are days still to come. A pin marks today.

  • 75 and above: on track.
  • 50 to 74: above usual pace.
  • Below 50: a tight month.

Free forever. On Pro, the cycle also follows the day your salary actually lands, when it comes up to 5 days early or late.

Free

Ahead of pace, and the top action

Pace compares what you have spent on flexible things (food, rides, shopping) with what you usually spend by this day of the cycle. “₹1,770 ahead of pace” means you have spent that much more than usual so far. It is a plain diagnosis, not a judgement.

When something is worth doing, Pulse shows one suggested action: a category drifting up, a subscription to review, a possible duplicate charge, a run of small spends. Only the most useful one is shown. When nothing is drifting, Pulse says so and stays quiet.

The top action is free. Pro shows all the suggestions, with the evidence behind each one.

Free

Run-short warning

If your pace means you may run short before payday, Spends tells you plainly. This warning is never behind a paywall. On the tight-month screen below, the line reads “At this pace, salary exhausts in ~5 days.”

Always free. Pro adds how much, which day, and what to do about it.

Can I buy?

Tap “Can I buy?” on Pulse and type a price. Spends checks it against the flexible money left in this cycle, not your bank balance (which still has rent in it). The answer is one of: buy now, wait, exceeds budget, or not enough data.

It is an estimate from your own data, not financial advice, and the sheet says so.

Free: 7 checks in your first 90 days. Pro: unlimited checks, with the effect on your goals.

Pro

Payday cash forecast

The forecast estimates the lowest your cash is likely to fall before payday: cash now, minus bills due before payday, minus your daily pace for the days left. It is rule-based, and the answer is a band, never a percentage or a probability:

  • Safe: comfortably above your safety floor.
  • Watch: within about a week of flexible spending of your floor.
  • At risk: below your floor.

It needs a safety floor (the least you want in the bank) and a recent balance. The balance can come from your bank alerts (“Avl bal”) or one you type. If the balance is more than 72 hours old, capture hasn't run for a day, there is less than a week of pace, or no floor is set, the card says “Not enough data” and tells you what to add, instead of guessing.

Pro. Also on Pro: a next-7-days outlook, your card bill so far, and the room left in each account before payday.

Free

Activity and corrections

Activity lists every transaction Spends found: merchant, time, account and category. Switch between this cycle and all time, search by merchant or amount, and filter.

  • Tap a spend to fix its merchant or category, or to mark it as not counted.
  • Tap + to add a cash spend or anything the alerts missed.
  • Share a bank alert from any app into Spends, and it is read once on the phone.

Your corrections are kept as your own record. Spends never overwrites what you told it.

Free forever: viewing, editing and adding.

Insights and Trends

Insights answers “where did it go?” for this cycle: each category against its usual amount, with the same day cells as the cycle rail, and a short summary in plain words.

“By month” shows Trends by calendar month. Part months are marked and kept out of comparisons.

This cycle is free. Pro adds every month side by side (a typical month, the highest and the lowest), the places you return to, a year in review and a monthly PDF report, all made on your phone.

Subscriptions

Spends finds subscriptions from evidence, strongest first:

  • the bank alert says AutoPay, e-mandate or standing instruction;
  • a known service such as Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, JioHotstar or Google One;
  • the same merchant charging a steady amount on a steady rhythm, three times or more.

Food orders, rides and SIPs are never called subscriptions. You can confirm one, mark it “not a subscription”, or say you cancelled it. If it charges again after that, Spends says “charged after you cancelled”.

The list, with its monthly and yearly total, is free. Pro adds alerts: a renewal reminder 2 days before, price changes and missed charges.

Free

Wallet and balances from alerts

Wallet shows your bank accounts and cards as cards, with what each spent this cycle. Many bank alerts include your available balance, so Spends shows it with where it came from: “Balance ₹48,211 · from HDFC alert, 2 h ago”.

A card Spends hasn't placed yet waits under “Unsorted cards”. Drop it on the account that pays it, or tap it, and its spending rolls into that account. A credit card's “available limit” is never read as a balance.

Free. Pro adds the room left in each account before payday.

Free

Refunds and duplicates

  • The same payment often arrives twice: an SMS from your bank and a notification from your UPI app. Spends merges them into one.
  • A spend seen only in a notification shows as pending until the bank confirms it.
  • A refund is linked to the purchase it came from (within 30 days). A partial refund lowers that spend; a full refund takes it out of your totals.
  • Failed and declined payments are skipped, so they never count as spending.
  • A possible duplicate charge (the same amount, same merchant, within 15 minutes) is flagged for you to check, not removed.

Free.

Free

Own-account transfers are not spending

Moving money between your own accounts isn't spending. When the same amount leaves one of your accounts and arrives in another within 2 days, Spends treats it as a transfer. Paying your credit-card bill from your bank account isn't counted again either, because the card spends were already counted. If Spends gets one wrong, mark it “count this” or “don't count this”, and your choice wins.

Free.

Weekly check and habit streaks

Once a week, a one-minute check: the last 7 days against a usual week, why, one useful action, and “Did this show you something useful?”. Your streak counts the weeks you kept. One pause week is allowed in any four, and a missed week is never shown as a loss.

The weekly check and streak are free. Pro adds weekly reminders (on Sundays).

FreeMore in Pro

Home-screen widget

A small widget with your days to payday. It is never used to nag.

Days to payday is free. Pro adds the payday forecast band to the widget.

Free

Export and delete

Export every transaction to CSV or JSON from Settings, whenever you like. Settings → Delete all data erases everything Spends stored on the phone. If you made an account, Settings → Account → Delete account removes it from our server too.

Free forever, including if you ever stop paying for Pro.

Free

App lock

Turn on App lock in Settings to open Spends with your fingerprint or face. Money screens also block screenshots and hide their contents in the recent-apps view.

Free.

Coming

Your own encrypted backups

A backup you own: saved to a folder in your own Google Drive, or to a file on your phone. It is encrypted on the phone with AES-256, using a passphrase you set. Spends can never read it.

  • Restore needs the same Google account that made the backup, and the passphrase.
  • If the passphrase is forgotten, the backup can't be opened. The app will say so when you set it.
  • Always optional. Spends never makes a backup unless you turn it on.

Coming. Today, Android cloud backup is off for Spends, so data does not move to a new phone by itself.

Coming

More banks, and instant capture

Today Spends recognises about 20 Indian banks by their SMS sender, plus UPI apps and wallets. Messages from other banks are read with general patterns, which can miss the merchant name. More banks are being added from testers' reports.

Today, new bank SMS are picked up when you open Spends. Instant capture, the moment an SMS arrives, is coming.
