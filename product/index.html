Know if your salary will last until payday.

Spends reads your bank and UPI alerts on your phone, works out your pace against payday, and tells you one clear thing to do. Your spending is never uploaded.

All featuresHow it works

Privacy promise

Your spending stays on your phone.

  • Read on the phone. Spends reads bank and UPI alerts only. It skips OTPs, personal chats and contacts.
  • Never uploaded. Your transactions, SMS and notifications are not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Stored encrypted. Data is encrypted on the phone, and Android cloud backup is off.
  • Yours to take or delete. Export to CSV or JSON, or delete everything, at any time. Free forever.
  • No lending. Spends never offers loans or credit, in any plan.
