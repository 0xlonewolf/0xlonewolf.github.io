The 90 days and the 7 free checks count from the day you first open Spends.

The trial

  • 30 days of Pro, free, once per Google account. You choose to start it; it never starts by itself.
  • The end date is shown before you start.
  • Reminders 7 days and 2 days before it ends, so it never renews silently.
  • When it ends without a subscription, you keep Free, all your data and automatic capture.

Cancel in one tap

Pro is billed through Google Play. Settings → Manage subscription takes you straight to Play, where you cancel in one tap. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then return to Free. Nothing you tracked is deleted.

What is never paid

  • Reading your bank alerts. Capture is free forever.
  • The warning that you may run short before payday.
  • Seeing, fixing, exporting or deleting your own data.

Spends never offers loans or credit, in any plan.
