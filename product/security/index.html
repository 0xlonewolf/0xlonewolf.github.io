Security and privacy

Your money data stays on your phone.

Spends reads your bank alerts on the device and keeps what it learns there, encrypted. Here is exactly what it reads, what it stores, what can leave the phone, and how to switch any of it off.

What Spends reads

  • Bank and UPI SMS about money: debits, credits, salary and balance alerts.
  • If you turn on notification access: alerts from an approved list of bank and payment apps. Others are ignored.
  • Alerts you choose to share into Spends, and spends you add yourself.

What it never reads

  • OTPs. They are rejected before anything is read from them.
  • Personal chats, social and marketing messages.
  • Your contacts.
  • Your bank login. There is none to give.

On the device only

Reading alerts, matching duplicates, scoring, forecasting and reports all run on your phone. Your transactions, SMS and notifications are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Spends uses no advertising and no third-party analytics.

Encrypted storage

  • On Android, Spends stores its data with AES-256-GCM encryption, using a key held by Android's keystore.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for Spends, so your data is not copied to cloud backups.
  • Full SMS and notification bodies are never stored. Spends keeps the details it reads (amount, merchant, account, date) and a snippet of up to 80 characters, so it can show you why it counted something.
  • New notifications wait briefly in the app's private storage until Spends processes them. That small queue is cleared when you delete your data.

Permissions, and how to revoke them

Spends never asks for a permission at startup. Each one is explained in the app first.

  • SMS: to read bank alerts. Turn it off in Android Settings → Apps → Spends → Permissions → SMS. What was already imported stays until you delete it. You can still add spends by hand or share alerts.
  • Notification access (optional): to read alerts from approved bank and UPI apps. Turn it off in Android Settings → Notification access → Spends. The exact menu names vary a little by phone.
  • Biometrics (optional): for App lock and to confirm a delete or export.
  • Internet: only for the optional account, the Pro waitlist and Google Play billing. Never for your spending data.

App lock and screen protection

Turn on App lock to open Spends with your fingerprint or face. Money screens block screenshots and hide their contents in the recent-apps view.

Diagnostics are opt-in

Spends keeps a diagnostic log on your phone to help fix problems. It contains no amounts, merchants, message text, names or account numbers. It leaves your phone only if you tap “Share diagnostic log” and choose where to send it. Usage counts stay on the phone.

The account is optional

You can use Spends without an account. If you choose Continue with Google, our server (Google Cloud, Mumbai, India) stores only your Google account ID, email, when your free period started, how many free purchase checks you have used, and your plan. Never your transactions.

Deleting everything

  • Settings → Delete all data erases everything Spends stored on the phone, including its keys and queues.
  • Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account from our server straight away.
  • You can also ask for account deletion on the web: Delete account.
  • Before you go, export your data to CSV or JSON from Settings. Free, always.

Backups you own Coming

Today there is no cloud backup. Coming next: an optional backup to a folder in your own Google Drive, or to a file on your phone.

  • Encrypted on the phone with AES-256 before it leaves, using a passphrase you set. Spends can never read it.
  • Spends can only write and read its own backup file in the folder you pick. Nothing else in your Drive.
  • Restore works only with the same Google account and the passphrase. A forgotten passphrase can't be recovered.
  • Never made unless you turn it on.
